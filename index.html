About Us

We are Project Verde, a student robotics team from R4Creating with over 20 years of experience in BEST Robotics. Project Verde was inspired by our New Mexican roots and the importance of green chile to our culture, agriculture, and economy.

We designed a robot to help automate food harvesting, preparation, and delivery for the Byte to Bite Café challenge. Our team builds on R4Creating's history of innovation with such projects as Arty, which helped train AI; Bishop, which worked on making lunar living possible; and Lily, which helped make heart surgery safer.

We bring together students from four counties with different grade levels, backgrounds, and skills to collaborate, solve problems, and turn ideas into reality. Beyond robotics, we believe in giving back to our community. This year, we participated in 50 community outreach activities including summer STEM camps, a free community STEM festival, and Rio Rancho's Mayor's Sunday Funday.

We are Project Verde: Growing Innovation. Serving Tomorrow.

BEST Robotics

BEST Robotics challenges middle and high school students to turn everyday materials into a fully functioning robot in just eight weeks! Through hand engineering, coding, building, and teamwork, students tackle real-world challenges while developing critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills. More than just a robotics competition, BEST empowers students to explore STEM careers, discover their potential, and become the next generation of innovators and technology leaders.
